# The boxed passage beside the description. Blank lines between paragraphs are kept.
excerpt: |+
  This isn’t exactly the way I expected freedom to feel. Never would any director in any movie direct this scene. Never would any set designer get away with this. The cinematographer would quit. No producer in their right mind would kick off the life of a capable, eager protagonist like me washed out in semi-flickering harsh fluorescent lighting. A single overhead light casting shadows on everything and stripping me of all my color.
  
  I can just see it.

---

A semi-autobiographical novel, written in sporadic sprints, mostly at night.

Writing as a way of processing emotions is underrated. This started that way and grew into something I want to put out into the world. Too many people struggling with mental health feel alone. I've been there, and I felt alone too. Besides being my way of working through that time in my life, I hope this lets anyone struggling know they are not alone.
