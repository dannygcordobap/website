## Where are you from?

... is a complicated question for me. We moved around a lot growing up.

***"So do you speak ____?"***

English and Spanish, yes. But to answer your real question: no, I do not speak German or Japanese.

I know what you are thinking: wasted opportunity. Now that I am older and more mature, I agree.

Kids will be kids.

## What's your story?

A lot of us were told that if you liked math and science and solving hard problems, you should become an engineer. Usually followed by the obligatory "and it pays well too!"

For as long as I can remember, I've enjoyed solving problems and mixing math and science with creativity.

So I went into engineering. 

I didn't love it, but I did love coding. It felt like the thing all those grown-ups had been describing. They just didn't know it.

So then I became a software engineer. 

It was ok. I wanted to make more decisions and have more autonomy. I wanted to build something.

So then I became a founder.

And I finally found what they were talking about all those years ago.
